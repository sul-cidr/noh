Shōdan in Okina

Okina Prayer

Voice
Non-congruent
Percussion
Strict

An arhythmic prayer performed in sashinori and einori, sung in the tsuyogin style. While Okina’s first prayer celebrates the deities, the second refers to a crane uttering the celebratory word manzai, meaning “ten thousand years,” and to a tortoise’s backbone, which symbolizes the completeness of the universe: Heaven, Earth, and Man.

In principle, percussion does not play in this shōdan. The pattern at the end of Okina Prayer 1 (measure 3) may therefore be read as the run-up strokes leading into Response to Okina, the shōdan that follows without a break.
