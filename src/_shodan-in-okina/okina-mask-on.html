Shōdan in Okina

Putting on the mask

In the play Okina, both Okina-to-be and Sanbasō-to-be don masks on stage, an act that signifies their transformation from mortal beings into deities. Okina puts on his mask during Senzai's second dance, Senzai-no-mai; because the two overlap, this act could not be noted in this collection as a separate shōdan section. The Sanbasō's mask-donning, though understated, does stand as its own shōdan: he moves upstage and, assisted by a kōken, dons his mask facing away from the audience, with no musical accompaniment.
