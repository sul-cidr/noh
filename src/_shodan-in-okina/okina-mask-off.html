Shōdan in Okina

Taking off the mask

This last section brings the ritual to a close as Sanbasō removes his mask, just as unceremoniously as he put it on. Kneeling with his back to the audience, he takes off the mask and bows to it, ceasing to be Sanbasō and returning to being an old man. He then slides across the stage before standing up and leaving.
