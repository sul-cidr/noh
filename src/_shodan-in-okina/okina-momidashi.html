Shōdan in Okina

Momidashi

Voice
Unmetered
Percussion
Strict
Nohkan
Non-congruent

This is a dance-to-text, the first shōdan of Okina’s second act. It is consists of three parts, the first two being exclusively instrumental, the third one involving singing and movement:

Part I Sanbasō enters the stage as the nohkan is performing its Momidashi pattern. It lasts seven honji.

Part II Sanbasō sits at Square 1. It lasts fifteen honji.

Part III Sanbasō stands and goes to Square 3 to deliver its text. It lasts twelve honji

The nohkan opens the shōdan with its hishigi pattern, followed by the momidashi pattern, which consists of a single phrase that begins in the high register with the pattern "O-hya." This phrase is performed as Sanbasō sits at Square 1, framing Part I and marking the beginning of Part II, which is played exclusively by percussion.

Throughout the shōdan, the three kotsuzumi players perform the four-beat pattern shown below, while the ōtsuzumi interjects strokes, often off the beat, creating an engaging rhythmic texture. This pattern was heard before in Senzai Song 2 and Senzai-no-mai 2.
