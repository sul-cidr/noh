      <h2>Shōdan in Okina</h2>
      <p>
        <em>Shōdan</em> are recurring modules that appear across Noh plays. At
        the same time, a <em>shōdan</em> can be understood as a component of a
        <em>dan</em>, the larger unit corresponding to a shared event or
        dramatic scene in a play’s progression. Taking this latter view,
        <em>Okina</em> can likewise be divided into <em>shōdan</em> grouped into
        larger <em>dan</em>. Because the <em>shōdan</em> on this page belong
        exclusively to <em>Okina</em>, we forgo a general explanation of the
        form here and focus instead on the musical aspects of each
        <em>shōdan</em>. The cards below lead to pages with musical analysis;
        follow the link at the bottom of each to reach the Aspects and
        Intermedia sections, where you can learn more about the
        <em>shōdan</em>’s other elements and their combined dramatic effect.
      </p>
