Catalog of Shōdan: OKINA

Okina / Nanori

Voice
Unmetered

The Nanori is a prose speech in which a character identifies himself and explains the reason for his presence at the location. In the case of Okina, it is delivered by Furyū Senzai, who reminds the audience that the event commemorates the relocation of Kyoto City University of Arts.
