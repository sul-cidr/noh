Catalog of Shōdan: OKINA

Okina / Prayer

Voice
Non-congruent

An arhythmic prayer performed in sashinori, sung in the tsuyogin style. While Okina’s first prayer celebrates the deities, the second one alludes to the fundamental Shinto-Buddhist trinity, symbolizing the completeness of the universe: Heaven, Earth, and Man.
