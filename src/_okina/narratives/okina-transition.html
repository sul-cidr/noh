Synopsis
With the Okina act concluded, the stage undergoes a technical transition in preparation for the Furyū act. The head kōken, his duty of handling the sacred mask complete, exits through the side door as the assistant kōken moves into the primary position behind the mask box. A more telling change occurs with the ōtsuzumi player. Having performed a minor role while seated on the floor, he now moves to a small chair, taking a position equal to the kotsuzumi players. This repositioning signals a significant shift in the musical dynamic, anticipating his more prominent role in the act to follow.
