Take off the mask
Synopsis
Sanbasō removes the mask.
Text
N/A
Music
N/A
Visuals
Kneeling at Square 4 Sanbasō removes his mask.

The removing of the mask is made without any ceremony, the same it was put on. Sanbasō, kneeling at Square 7, with his back to the public, removes his mask, bow to it 1’23’24” and ceased to be Sanbasō, thus returning to be an old man. He then slides three times once to the left, then to the right, and one last time to the left before standing up and leaving the stage.
