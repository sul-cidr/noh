Index of Intermedia

The overall Index of Intermedia (IoI) of the third and final dan of the second act consists of two shōdan, maintaining the IoI at its highest level for most of the section.

This last section brings the ritual to a close, as Sanbasō removes his mask before leaving the stage. This unaccompanied, unceremonious moment brings the IoI to its lowest level.


The removing of the mask is made without any ceremony, the same it was put on. Sanbasō, kneeling at Square 7, with his back to the public, removes his mask, bows to it, and ceased to be Sanbasō, thus returning to be an old man. He then slides three times once to the left, then to the right, and one last time to the left before standing up and leaving the stage.
