Form
Okina-Okina prays:
Prayer-1 → Response → Prayer-2
Synopsis
This is the first subsection of Okina’s fifth section. This is an unaccompanied song prayer from Okina to the Gods.
Text
Arhythmic. The text, set in sashinori is sung in tsuyogin style by Okina.
Music
N/A
Visuals
Okina stands at Square 8 with extended arms as he offers a prayer to the Gods.

Returning to Square 8, Okina, after extending his arms, delivers a three-honji unaccompanied prayer celebrating the gods.
