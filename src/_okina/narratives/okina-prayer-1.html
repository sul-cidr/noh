Form
Okina-Okina Prays:
Okina Prayer 1 → Response to Okina → Okina Prayer 2
Synopsis
After creating a moment of anticipation by slowly opening his arms, Okina offers his first prayer. Celebrating the gods, his words are delivered as a slow, powerful incantation, establishing a deeply sacred and magical atmosphere.
Text
Arhythmic. The text, set in einori and sashinori is sung in tsuyogin style by Okina.
Music
N/A
Visuals
Okina stands at Square 8 with extended arms as he offers the prayer.
Index of Intermedia

The overall Index of Intermedia (IoI) of this fifth dan, which comprises six shōdan, rises to the first act’s highest level following a steady decrease.

The first shōdan features an arhythmic song performed by Okina and the jiutai, supported by the ōtsuzumi, three kotsuzumi drums in a strict rhythmic setting and a non-congruent nohkan part. Its IoI, however, remains unchanged from the preceding shōdan, staying at a low midrange level.


This shōdan has a very low Index of Intermedia (IoI), consisting almost entirely of Okina's unaccompanied solo voice. The performance begins with a moment of visual anticipation as Okina standing at Square 8 slowly opens his arms before uttering the prayer. His words, which celebrate the gods, are delivered like a magical incantation: very slowly and with the highly decorative melismas of the Einori style. A seamless bridge to the next section is created as the music for the following shōdan—featuring the nohkan and a single kotsuzumi drum—begins over the final word of the prayer, signaling an imminent rise in the IoI.
