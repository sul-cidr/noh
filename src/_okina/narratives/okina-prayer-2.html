Form
Okina-Okina Prays:
Okina Prayer 1 → Response to Okina → Okina Prayer 2
Synopsis
Okina delivers his second prayer, invoking themes from the celebratory gagaku piece Manzairaku and the cosmic harmony symbolized by an ancient tortoise bearing Heaven, Earth, and Man upon its back. The prayer builds in intensity, culminating in a powerful proclamation as he asks for peace and security for all under heaven.
Text
Arhythmic. The text, set in sashinori is sung in tsuyogin style by Okina.
Music
N/A
Visuals
Standing on Square 8, Okina's movements are deliberate and contained. Before singing, he slowly opens his arms, creating a moment of visual focus. As he delivers the prayer, he holds a simple pose, with his right arm lightly raised while holding his fan.
Index of Intermedia

The overall Index of Intermedia (IoI) of this fifth dan, which comprises six shōdan, rises to the first act’s highest level following a steady decrease.

The third shōdan, an unaccompanied sashinori chant performed by Okina and the jiutai, brings the IoI to the dan’s lowest level.


The Index of Intermedia (IoI) returns to very low as Okina once again sings his prayer alone and unaccompanied. Following the jiutai’s response in the previous shōdan, Okina creates a moment of visual anticipation by slowly opening his arms. His prayer invokes powerful symbols of longevity and cosmic harmony: the thousand-year crane singing of Manzairaku (Ten Thousand Years of Happiness), and the ancient tortoise bearing the three poles of Heaven, Earth, and Man. By invoking Manzairaku, a piece of celebratory dance music, the prayer thematically prepares the way for the dance dan that is to follow. The vocal delivery itself has a dynamic arc, building from an introspective prayer into a higher pitched and louder, powerful proclamation for the final lines: "All under heaven at peace, the land secure." Just as in the first prayer, a smooth bridge to the next section is created as the music for the following shōdan begins over the final word, signaling an imminent rise in the IoI.
