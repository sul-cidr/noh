Visuals
After removing his mask, Okina returns being an old man, who exits the stage after bowing to the audience.

After having removed his mask, the old man goes to center stage, Square 8 where he performs a variation on the closure scoop 37’07”. Having bowed to the audience 37’44”, he slowly walks toward the bridge. During this entire sequence, the three kotsuzumi players perform a single eight-beat pattern, focusing like many preceding patterns on beats: 1, 3, 5, and 7.

Figure 12

As he reaches approximately the midpoint of the bridge, the pattern accelerates to twice its initial tempo, transitioning to a four-beat pattern, seemingly propelling Okina, carrying the old man, off the stage. The subsequent figure, which illustrates a sequence of two such patterns, demonstrates how beats 1, 3, 5, and 7 are each preceded by a pick-up.
