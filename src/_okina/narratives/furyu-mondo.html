Form
Furyū-Dialogue:
Mondō 1
Synopsis
Pine and Bamboo introduce themselves to Furyū Senzai and remind us of the symbolism associated with them.
Text
Spoken prose
Music
Unaccompanied vocal part delivered in kotoba.
Visuals
During the dialogue, Furyū Senzai remains on Square 8, while Pine and Bamboo enter the stage.
Index of Intermedia

The Index of Intermedia (IoI) of this third dan of the Furyū consists of a single shōdan, which substantially lowers the level of the IoI.

This shōdan features an unaccompanied dialogue between the kyōgen, Bamboo, and Pine, bringing the IoI to one of its lowest points in the Furyū.


During the initial portion of this unaccompanied dialogue, Bamboo and Pine remain on the bridge, informing Furyūsenzai of their arrival to celebrate, through music and dance, the relocation of the Kyoto City University of Arts.

Following an invitation from Furyūsenzai to join him on stage, Pine and Bamboo take their positions on Squares 2 and 6, respectively. Upon being requested to introduce themselves, they rotate to face the audience, explaining their symbolic association with longevity. At the dialogue's conclusion, Furyūsenzai extends an invitation for them to participate in this celebration with a dance.
