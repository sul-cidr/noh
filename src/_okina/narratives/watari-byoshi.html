Form
Furyū-Furyūsenzai Enters:
Watari byōshi → Noriji-1 → Nanori
Synopsis
The Watari-byōshi is brief entrance music that brings the character FuryūSenzai, a kyōgen actor, onto the stage. He is an unexpected visitor who will help the celebration by bringing auspicious spirits.
Text
N/A
Music
The taiko, ōtsuzumi and the leader of the kotsuzumi are in strict, and the nohkan in congruent rhythmic settings.
Visuals
The visual action is a standard Noh entrance performed by FuryūSenzai. He enters the stage toward the end of the third honji and reaches Square 1 by the end of the fifth. Once there, he performs a Forward Point to Square 2 before immediately returning to Square 1, where he awaits the next part of the performance.

This concise entrance music has a medium Index of Intermedia, characterized by the full participation of the hayashi and congruent setting of the nohkan. For the first time, the taiko drum joins the ensemble, adding its weight to the watari-byōshi rhythmic pattern performed by the percussion. The nohkan reinforces this tight, rhythmic feel by playing a simple ABAC structure of patterns in a powerful congruent setting, which locks it directly with the drums. This synchronized interaction creates a straightforward and celebratory atmosphere, more akin to an informal kyōgen performance than the solemnity of the preceding act. The unusual richness of this musical setting for an aikyōgen (interlude) clearly signals that this is a performance for a special occasion.
