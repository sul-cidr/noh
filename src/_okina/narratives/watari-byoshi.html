Form
Furyū-Furyūsenzai Enters:
Watari byōshi → Noriji-1 → Nanori
Synopsis
This is an entrance music for Senzai Furyū. This is the first section to include the taiko and the nohkan in congruent rhythmic setting.
Text
N/A
Music
The Taiko, Ōtsuzumi and Kotsuzumi leader are in strict, and the nohkan in congruent rhythmic settings.
Visuals
The entrance music supports Senzai Furyū’s entrance.

Okina’s watari-byōshi is a concise entrance composition consisting of only seven honji, performed to welcome the character Senzai Furyū onto the stage. The taiko joins the hayashi, performing in conjunction with the ōtsuzumi and the leading kotsuzumi player a rhythmic pattern identified as watari-byōshi. Once again, this pattern emphasizes beats 1, 3, 5, and 7. The taiko and ōtsuzumi participate in the first four beats, while the second half features exclusively the kotsuzumi.

Figure 14

The nohkan returns, presenting a congruent pattern composed of four phrases, exhibiting an A B A C formal structure. Each phrase, comprised of three patterns, commences with the same shōga: Hiu-ya ra.

The kata performed during the entrance are standard. Senzai Furyū enters the stage and halts at Square 1, prior to executing a Forward point that takes him to Square 2, followed by a return to Square 1.
