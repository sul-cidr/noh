Form
Okina-Okina prays:
Prayer-1 → Response → Prayer-2
Synopsis
This is the second subsection of Okina’s fifth section. This is a very short subsection of only two-honji sung by the jiutai who reacts to Okina’s prayer.
Text
Arhythmic. The text, set in sashinori is sung in tsuyogin style by the jiutai.
Music
The kotsuzumi part performed by the lead player is in flexible, and the nohkan in non-congruent rhythmic settings.
Visuals
Okina standing on Square 8, performs a small Zigzag while the jiutai sings its response. This is the first kata performed by Okina. It anticipates his coming dances.

This two-honji passage is designated as a subsection due to its marked contrast with the preceding prayer. It also signals the return of the percussion and nohkan players, although only the lead kotsuzumi player participates in this subsection. This marks the first instance of the lead kotsuzumi material being presented in a flexible rhythmic setting.

The two-honji response, delivered by the jiutai, is characterized by a slow and introspective quality. It is supported by the nohkan's Sō no Shingon no te pattern. Its main sounds being in the instrument’s lower register, the pattern is well fitting the section’s overall reflective mood.

During this time, Okina executes a small zigzag movement, seemingly acknowledging the gods from all directions, thereby contributing to a sudden and substantial increase in the IoI. He concludes the response by once again bringing his hands together and covering his face with his opened fan.
