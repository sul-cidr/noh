Mondō 2
Synopsis
Sanbasō calls for an Ado, a term used in Kyōgen to identify a contrasting character. Thus, Senzai appears and encourages Sanbasō to dance as a prayer for blessings to ten thousand years.

Index of Intermedia

The Index of Intermedia (IoI) of this second dan of the second act, consists of a single shōdan, although it raises the IoI, it remains at a low level.

This shōdan features an unaccompanied dialogue between Sanbasō and Senzai.


Following the suspended stillness of Sanbasō's mask-donning, this new dan opens quietly: an unaccompanied dialogue between Sanbasō and Senzai that raises the Index of Intermedia only slightly, keeping it at a low level. Positioned at Square 1, Sanbasō alternates his gaze to the left and right, calling for an Ado. Senzai, located at Square 5, responds to this summons. They then engage in dialogue while remaining in their respective positions. At the dialogue's conclusion, Senzai presents Sanbasō with a suzu bell — the object at the heart of the ritual dance that follows.
