Form
Furyū-Furyūsenzai Enters:
Watari-byōshi → Noriji-1 → Nanori
Synopsis
Standing at Square 1, Furyūsenzai delivers a celebratory chant that affirms the auspicious nature of the event. He declares that the reign is peaceful and that tribulations ('waves and wind') are quiet. He then uses the image of "flowers to adorn our hair" as a joyful metaphor to announce that a special and propitious moment has arrived, concluding with unwavering confidence in the prevailing good fortune.
Text
Irregular rhythm. The text, set in ōnori is sung in tsuyogin style by Furyūsenzai
Music
The Taiko, Ōtsuzumi and Kotsuzumi leader are in strict rhythmic settings.
Visuals
The visual action in this shōdan is minimal and static. Furyūsenzai remains standing at Square 1 throughout the entire section. His focus is entirely on the delivery of his chant, with no associated dance or significant gestural movement.
Index of Intermedia

The overall Index of Intermedia (IoI) of the ‘Furyūsenzai Enters’ dan, comprising three shōdan, rises across its first two shōdan before dropping sharply in its last.

The second shōdan raises the IoI further, as FuryūSenzai’s chant joins the congruent percussion ostinato in a tight, noriji-style setting of text and music.


The Index of Intermedia is higher in this second shōdan of the 'Furyūsenzai Enters' dan, as Furyūsenzai's chant in the rhythmic ōnori style joins the congruent rhythmic ostinato established by the percussion. This sonic energy is set against a completely static visual backdrop, as Furyūsenzai remains standing at Square 1 throughout. The performance demonstrates a tight, expressive link between text and music, with the chant following the noriji structure (lines commencing on the second beat and syllables aligning one-per-beat). Crucially, the rhythm is used for emphasis, as the percussion deliberately decelerates to draw attention to key phrases. For instance, the rhythm slows to highlight the confident declaration “Auspicious indeed! The reign is at peace” at 42’38”, and again for the joyful conclusion, “...are in bloom! Indeed, it is so!” at 43’17”.
