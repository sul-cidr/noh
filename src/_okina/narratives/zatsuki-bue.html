Watari → Zatsuki-bue
Synopsis
The zatsuki-bue is a unique and ritualistic nohkan flute solo. It opens the play and is only performed for Okina. It is highly distinctive and is imbued with auspicious meaning.

Visuals
The solo is performed as everybody is settling on stage. It sets a solemn and mystical atmosphere, heightening the ceremonial nature of the performance.

The zatsuki-bue, a brief solo of approximately fifty seconds, is traditionally considered to 'purify the world,' thereby establishing the context for the sacred ritual of Okina. Once all participants have assumed their positions on stage, the nohkan player concludes the zatsuki-bue with the Hishigi pattern 11’44”, characterized by its high and penetrating tone, it functions as an invocation to the deities.
