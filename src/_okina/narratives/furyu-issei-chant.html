Form
Furyū-Furyū Enters:
Entrance music → Issei chant
Synopsis
Pine and Bamboo credit the reign for the current favorable conditions and wish for ten thousand more years.
Text
Poetic text in regular rhythm
Music
The Ōtsuzumi and Kotsuzumi leader are in strict rhythmic settings.
Visuals
Bamboo and Pine deliver this chant standing respectively at the First and Second Pines, while Furyū Senzai standing at Square 8, turns to face them, in preparation for the coming dialogue.

During the brief, four-honji einori chant, the repeated pattern from the noriji, performed by the kotsuzumi and ōtsuzumi, is sustained as the Bamboo and Pine characters offer their benedictions while remaining on the bridge. First, the spirit of the pine credits the current favorable conditions to the reign. Then, the spirit of the bamboo joins in as they express faith in continuing prosperity for ten thousand more years. The duet of the pine and bamboo spirits is essentially a wish for a long, healthy, and prosperous life for everyone. It is more than mere wishes, it is a prayer offered to the gods, making it a sacred part of the celebration.
