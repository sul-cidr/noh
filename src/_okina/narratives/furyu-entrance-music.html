Furyū-Furyū enters:
Entrance music → Issei chant
Synopsis
This entrance music welcomes on the bridge two characters personifying a pine and a bamboo tree.
Text
Short spoken introduction
Music
The Ōtsuzumi and Kotsuzumi leader are in strict rhythmic settings.
Visuals
Pine and Bamboo enter the bridge.

The concise, five-honji entrance music is scored for the leading kotsuzumi and ōtsuzumi. Reminiscent of a mitsu-ji pattern, the ōtsuzumi's stroke appears in the first half of the one-honji pattern, with the kotsuzumi's three strokes in the second half. The repeated combined pattern emphasizes beats 3, 5, 7, and 8.

Figure 15

It serves to welcome onto the stage two kyōgen actors who personify the spirits of the bamboo and pine trees. Within Japanese culture, bamboo symbolizes resilience, flexibility, and prosperity, while the pine tree symbolizes longevity and strength. After entering the bridge and standing at the First and Second Pines, respectively, they acknowledge Furyū Senzai by pointing their closed fans towards him 45’10”, prior to sliding back left to face the audience.
