Form
Okina-Senzai performs:
Song-1 → Dance-1 → Song-2 → Dance-2
Synopsis
Senzai Song-2 is the third work in the Section’s third subsection. Senzai, hoping for thousand autumns of peace and prosperity, remains worried about nature’s unpredictable power and the presence of malevolent spirits.
Text
Arhythmic. The text, set in sashinori is sung in yowagin style by Senzai and the jiutai.
Music
The three kotsuzumi are in strict.
Visuals
Senzai performs most of his kata upstage on Square 8, but concludes Song-2 by moving to Square 4, in preparation for the Dance-2.

The nohkan's tacet section exhibits a decreased IoI (Index Of Intermedia), mirroring its behavior in Song-1. The portion of the text sung by Senzai expresses hope for flourishing despite the presence of booming water. The jiutai's response enters midway through the song, beginning on the fourth of six measures, reminding the listeners that 'unending water flows on, booming.'

The kata performed during the song are largely spatially contained, with movements concentrated in the upper body and the swirling of sleeves, frequently concluding with a pointing gesture. The dance's initial phrase extends over the first two of the song's six honji. The four-honji second section brings the dancer to the frontstage at Square 4, seemingly propelled by the three percussionists' new and faster two-beat pattern (chi – po), superimposed with a four-beat kakegoe pattern. The momentum is further reinforced by a tempo change from ♩ = 60 to a rate more than twice as fast, ♩ = 130.
