Form
Okina-Senzai Performs:
Senzai Song 1 → Senzai Dance 1 → Senzai Song 2 → Senzai Dance 2
Synopsis
Senzai Song 2 is the third shōdan of the acts third dan. Senzai, hoping for thousand autumns of peace and prosperity, remains worried about nature’s unpredictable power and the presence of malevolent spirits.
Text
Regular. The text, set in sashinori is sung in tsuyogin style by Senzai and the jiutai.
Music
The three kotsuzumi are in strict rhythmic setting.
Visuals
Senzai performs most of his kata upstage on Square 8, but concludes Song-2 by moving to Square 4, in preparation for the Dance-2.

As the nohkan stops playing, the Index of Intermedia for Senzai Song 2 drops to the same level as Senzai Song 1. However, the overall expressive energy remains high, driven by the fast tempo of the music and stage movement. Senzai’s text expresses a hope for a millenuim of flourishing and resilience, like that of a waterfall. Midway through the song, the jiutai responds, their lines affirming that 'unending water flows on, booming.' At this moment, Senzai repeats the mimetic kata from the first song: he bends down before rising onto his toes, embodying the power and momentum of water 18’04”.

The kata in this shōdan are largely contained, with movement concentrated in the upper body through swirling sleeves and pointing gestures. The initial phrase of movement corresponds to the first two of the song’s six honji. For the remaining four honji, Senzai moves to the front of the stage at Square 4, propelled by a dramatic shift in the percussion. The three drummers introduce a new, faster pattern of even-duration 'chi' and 'po' sounds, layered with a four-beat kakegoe pattern. This momentum is powerfully reinforced as the drumming tempo more than doubles.
