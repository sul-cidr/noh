Index of Intermedia

The Index of Intermedia (IoI) of this third dan, which comprises four shōdan, is characterized by an ascending progression followed by a sudden drop.

The fourth and final shōdan features a short, unaccompanied dialogue between the shite and waki, which substantially lowers the IoI to one of its lowest levels in the play. This could be read as a brief respite following the third shōdan’s dynamic chant.


The transition from the singing in the previous shōdan to the spoken text in this one indicates a shift in focus. It moves from the theme of the reign to Takasago and poetry, which becomes the main emphasis of the Shite's Performs dan that begins next.
